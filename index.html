📐 DESK READ: The week did what the curve was hedging against — SBP held at 11.5% on Monday, the Fed hiked on Wednesday and signalled more. The rate differential narrowed by 25bps to 750bps, but PKR did not blink (277.26, +Re0.01 Thursday) because the reserve story overwhelmed it: SBP reserves jumped $3.06bn to a record $21.389bn on Eurobond proceeds, total liquid $26.79bn. Two live questions for the desk today: (1) does the MoF pay up at the long end in the PIB auction with UST 10Y sitting at 5.00%, or reject again; (2) does SPI confirm the fuel shock — petrol rose ~12.5% and HSD ~14% in the first 16 days of September before yesterday's 43-paisa cut.
⚡ CLIENT TALKING POINTS: (1) Importers — spot stability is now backed by a genuine buffer; theoretical 3M forward premium still ~8% p.a., so staggered cover beats full cover. (2) Exporters — a hawkish Fed path argues for layering forward sales sooner rather than later; premia compress as the USD leg rises. (3) Corporates on KIBOR — the 1Y fixing was carrying an +86bps hike premium pre-MPC; post-hold, watch whether it retraces before you lock resets. (4) Institutional investors — today's PIB is the cleanest read on whether banks will extend duration at 12.0–12.5% with global term premia still rising.

  • Curve shape: 12M MTB cleared 11.99% on 2 Sep and 10Y secondary was ~12.30% on 11 Sep — roughly 31bps of term premium for nine extra years. That is thin compensation with the Fed signalling more hikes and UST 10Y at 5.00%.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR PAKISTAN
CHANNEL 1 — TERM PREMIUM
UST 10Y at a 19-year high re-prices the whole EM curve. Pakistan's fresh $3bn Eurobond (5.5Y and 10Y tranches) marks against that benchmark; spreads widen mechanically even with domestic fundamentals improving. Watch secondary levels before any follow-on issuance.
CHANNEL 2 — CARRY
Differential narrows to 750bps from 775bps. At 8% theoretical forward premia and a stable spot, PKR carry stays attractive to hedged holders. The reserve build ($21.39bn) is doing more for the currency than the differential is.
CHANNEL 3 — COMMODITIES
A firmer dollar is oil-negative at the margin, and Brent has now fallen two sessions on Saudi supply workarounds. If that holds into the OGRA window, the fuel-CPI impulse peaks this month rather than compounding into October — the single most helpful thing for the 26 Oct MPC.

CNBC · REUTERS VIA CNBC · BR · AAJ (16–18 SEP)

DOMESTIC POLICY
  • Austerity package (PM-chaired meeting): 50% cut in official vehicle fuel quotas, 5% cut in non-ERE budget FY27, ban on new government vehicle and durable-goods purchases, three-month suspension of overseas travel, markets to close 9pm and restaurants 11pm.
  • Fuel subsidy live: Rs100/L relief for motorcycles, rickshaws and vehicles up to 800cc; registration via 9771 with SMS charges waived; cost ~Rs25bn/month.
  • Pricing mechanism: the petroleum minister says cabinet has tasked OGRA with setting fuel prices on a daily basis off international trends — a structural change in how the CPI energy impulse arrives.
  • Taxes on fuel: Rs114/L on petrol and Rs100/L on diesel remain in place; separately BR reported an Rs80/L FED on three POL products ⏳.
  • SBP Governor (17 Sep): credited remittances for the reserve build-up and pointed to a contained current account; ~$3.5bn of FY27's $11bn net external repayments already done.
